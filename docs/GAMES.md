## Rocket science, for real

[Orbit Lab](https://normansrule.github.io/pixel-arcade/orbit/) is a Kerbal-style rocket game where the maths is on screen: Tsiolkovsky gives your Δv, orbits are real two-body physics with patched conics, and a Hohmann helper works out each transfer burn. Ten space cabinets teach one physics idea each: gravity slingshots, docking, transfer windows, landers on eight worlds, Kepler's third law, solar sails, asteroid deflection, launch control, relativistic navigation and Δv-budget cargo runs.
